About Happy Seeds

Gardening feels good. Buying seeds should too.

We started Happy Seeds because growing something brings people joy, and we wanted the moment you buy the seeds to feel that way as well.

Why we started

Joy is built into gardening.

There's a feeling that comes with growing something: the first green loop breaking through the soil, dirt under your fingernails, a tomato that's still warm from the sun. Nobody has to teach it to you. Gardening simply makes people happy.

Buying seeds rarely felt that way. It meant a crowded rack, tiny print and a lot of guessing about what would actually grow. The joy was waiting at the end, but the beginning felt like a chore.

So we set out to change the beginning. Happy Seeds exists to make the experience of buying seeds represent, reflect and evoke the same feeling as the garden itself, from the moment you see the box to the day you pick your first harvest.

Meet the Happy Farmer

And his hummingbird, Layla.

The Happy Farmer lives in a world where the plants tower over him, and he wouldn't have it any other way. He waters the seedlings, sows the rows and stops to admire every new leaf, with Layla perched on his hat holding a little yellow flower.

They're the heart of Happy Seeds: a reminder that a garden is a small, wonderful world, and that you get to step into it the moment you open the box.
