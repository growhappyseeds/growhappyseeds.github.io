Housewarming Gifts for a Gardener: What to Give a New Home

By Happy Seeds · November 12, 2026 · 6 min read

What to give someone who just moved and wants to grow things, sorted by the home they moved into.

The short answer The best housewarming gift for a gardener is one that waits until they are ready: a complete vegetable seed kit, a good pair of pruners or a set of pots for the space they actually have. Skip live plants that need care in the first week and anything that assumes a yard they may not have.

A housewarming gift for a gardener has one job that other gifts do not: it has to wait. The person you are giving it to is living among boxes, does not yet know where the sun falls in the new yard, and may not know whether the soil is clay or sand. The best gifts are the ones that are still useful in three months, when the garden actually starts.

This guide sorts gifts by the home they moved into, because an apartment with a balcony and a house with a quarter acre need different things. Most ideas are under $25; a few are a little more. For gifts aimed at someone who is brand new to growing rather than someone who has moved, see Gifts for New Gardeners.

The one rule: give something that can wait

A gift that needs attention in the first week after a move is a chore, not a present. Live plants in particular suffer: they ride in a hot car, sit in a hallway with no light, and get watered when someone remembers. Seed, tools, pots and books keep without any care at all.

Seed in particular keeps for years when stored cool, dry and dark, so a seed kit given in November is still good when the ground thaws. That also means you do not need to time the gift to the planting season. Give it when the housewarming happens and let the recipient plant it when they are ready.

For an apartment or a balcony

Give containers and seed that grows in them. A balcony garden fails when the pots are too small for the crop, so the gift should solve that. Two or three fabric or plastic pots, one of them 5 gallons, cover the fruiting crops; a window box or a 12 inch deep trough covers greens and roots.

Pair the pots with a seed kit that says what size pot each crop needs. Greens such as spinach and kale and roots such as beets and turnips are happy in 10 to 12 inches of soil. Peppers want 3 to 5 gallons, tomatoes, cucumbers and eggplant want 5 gallons, and a melon needs 10 gallons or more. The Best Vegetables to Grow on a Balcony goes through sun, wind and watering for small spaces if you want to add a note.

A soil thermometer is the small extra that balcony growers rarely buy for themselves. Pots warm up and dry out faster than ground soil, and a thermometer tells them when the soil is warm enough for cucumbers and melons.

For a rental with a small yard

Renters hesitate to dig, so give things that work above ground or come with them when they leave. A raised bed kit that assembles without tools is the generous option. A set of large fabric pots is the budget version and moves just as easily as the furniture.

With the bed or the pots, add seed for a first season. A kit with a mix of cool-season and warm-season crops gives them something to plant in early spring and again in late summer, which matters when they do not know yet how long they will stay. A kit that holds enough seed for a second sowing is better than one that is used up in one go.

For a house with a real garden for the first time

This is the person who has never had ground to dig and is about to buy everything at once. Give the two tools they will use most: a hand trowel with a solid metal blade and a pair of bypass pruners from a maker that sells replacement blades. Cheap versions of both bend or jam in the first season.

Then give them the plan. A complete vegetable seed kit with a grow guide covers what to plant, how deep, how far apart and when, relative to their last frost date. The guide matters more in a new place than anywhere else, because the recipient has no experience of this yard's frost dates or soil. Whatever the guide says, tell them to confirm their frost dates with their county extension office; the state planting pages on this site give a starting window for every state.

For the plant lover who grows indoors

Not everyone who gardens has outdoor space, and a move often means a new set of windowsills. A grow light on a timer is the gift that makes a north-facing kitchen workable. A set of seed-starting trays with a clear dome lets them start tomatoes and peppers indoors in late winter, which is when a new garden really begins. When to Start Seeds Indoors covers the timing if you want to tuck a note in.

Seed still fits here. A kit's spinach, kale and beets grow on a bright sill, and the warm-season crops can be started indoors and moved out when the weather allows.

What to skip

Skip a tender houseplant unless you know where it will sit and that someone will water it. Skip anything that assumes a yard: a wheelbarrow, a hose reel, a bag of compost. Skip gift cards to a nursery that is near their old home rather than their new one. Skip seed packets with no variety name, which tell the grower nothing about days to maturity or whether the crop fits a pot.

And skip the ornamental seed bomb or novelty kit that grows one marigold in a tin. It is a pleasant object, but it is not a garden, and a gardener will know the difference.

Common questions

Is a seed kit a good housewarming gift?

Yes, for one reason above all: it does not need anything from the recipient on moving day. Seed keeps for years in a cool, dry drawer, so the kit waits until the boxes are unpacked and the frost dates are known. A kit with a grow guide also answers the questions a new gardener in a new place will have.

What is a good housewarming gift for a gardener under $25?

A complete vegetable seed kit, a hand trowel with a metal blade, a soil thermometer, a pair of bypass pruners from a known maker, or a set of labeled seed packets chosen for their climate. The Happy Seeds Veggie Garden Seed Kit is $24.99 and includes ten varieties, 1,000+ seeds and a 24-page guide.

Should I give a live plant as a housewarming gift?

Only if you know they have a spot and the time for it in the first week. A plant handed over during a move often sits in a hot car or a dark hallway. A seed kit, pots or tools wait without harm. If you want to give something living, a hardy herb in a pot is safer than a tender houseplant.

What housewarming gift works for an apartment with a balcony?

Containers and seed that grow in them. Most greens and root crops grow in 10 to 12 inches of soil; a tomato or cucumber needs a 5 gallon pot. A seed kit that lists a pot size for each crop plus two or three fabric pots of the right size is a complete balcony garden in one gift.

When is the best time to give a gardening housewarming gift?

Any time, because seed and tools keep. If they move in autumn or winter, a seed kit gives them a project to plan over the cold months. If they move in spring, add a note with their local last frost date and tell them to confirm it with their county extension office before planting.
