## The Happy Farmer (Jared, Nov 1 2026)
The clay farmer in scene-farmer-shelf.png, made from the Happy Seeds mascot logo (cream cowboy hat, red bandana, mustache, white shirt, blue overalls), is the heart of the brand and is called **the Happy Farmer**. He lives inside the world of the plants: in creatives he is small, 10 to 20 percent of the plants' height, so the plants show at full scale and tower over him, and he is always admiring or tending what is growing, never selling. Lead with him in future creatives and marketing assets. First approved examples: Canva media MAHZvL8CIN4 (tomato plant), MAHZvPsBV9s (windowsill kale and spinach).

## Happy Farmer World (Jared, Nov 2 2026): the brand look
Every creative, site image and email header is a stop-motion claymation scene with the Happy Farmer inside the world of the plants: small (10-20% of plant height), always tending or admiring (watering, sowing, patting, pulling, harvesting, cooking, eating), and ALWAYS with the small green red-throated hummingbird from the logo perched on his hat brim. Matte plasticine with thumb marks, never photoreal food. The box appears only when the idea is about the product. Site set lives in media/site/farmer/ (hero, herbs, sowing, gift, sprout-wide, email-banner), social statics in media/2026-11/statics/farmer-*.jpg, Canva designs DAHY1SD7IiY (statics) and DAHZvdUR5_Y (site/email). Every image is also archived in the hello@ Drive folder "Happy Seeds Farmer World Claymation Images" (17gHsmP3cSui1OFsnOXUOv-13Zl12jJmZ), titled by its description.

## The box (Jared, Nov 2 2026): always portrait
When the box appears it is ALWAYS portrait oriented (standing tall, never landscape). The drawer slides out of a SHORT end, the black pull tab is centered on that short end (never on a long side), and the ten packets and the grow guide are displayed in portrait inside it. Packets are multi-colored like the real product, no readable words. Current open-box image: media/site/farmer/farmer-inside.jpg (Canva MAHZ05PqqfM, DAHZvdUR5_Y page 11; lid carries the real Veggie Garden front artwork, farmer calm and smiling, Jared Nov 2).

## Box lettering (Jared, Nov 2 2026): the logo is never messed up
Generators invent lettering on any box face they cannot copy. Rule: the box is shown square to the camera so only the FRONT face (the real Happy Seeds artwork, from reference MAHY1RbhAIs / MAHX10pD-6o) is visible; any sliver of side or top is plain yellow with no letters, words or marks. Any garbled, mirrored or invented text anywhere on the box, packets or guide fails review. Current wholesale image: media/site/farmer/farmer-wholesale.jpg (Canva MAHZyH8ei6c, DAHZvdUR5_Y page 8) replaced MAHZxX2Hgqw, which had scrambled text on the box side.

## Never invent a product (Jared, Nov 2 2026)
Never generate or show a box, packet, booklet, label or any packaging that is not the real Happy Seeds product photographed or approved by Jared ("they do not represent a product we carry, don't use this ever"). The Herb Garden Kit has no approved packaging yet: the herb kit page shows only the clay herbs, the Happy Farmer among herbs, and the real Veggie Garden Kit only in the section that links back to it. Product images come only from the approved references (MAHY1RbhAIs, MAHX10pD-6o, MAHX2DIl7cc, MAHX_FAoJrs, MAHX_Hbkx28, MAHZxhqXxE8, MAHZyH8ei6c).
