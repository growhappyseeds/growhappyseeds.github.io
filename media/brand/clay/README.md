## The Happy Farmer (Jared, Nov 1 2026)
The clay farmer in scene-farmer-shelf.png, made from the Happy Seeds mascot logo (cream cowboy hat, red bandana, mustache, white shirt, blue overalls), is the heart of the brand and is called **the Happy Farmer**. He lives inside the world of the plants: in creatives he is small, 10 to 20 percent of the plants' height, so the plants show at full scale and tower over him, and he is always admiring or tending what is growing, never selling. Lead with him in future creatives and marketing assets. First approved examples: Canva media MAHZvL8CIN4 (tomato plant), MAHZvPsBV9s (windowsill kale and spinach).
