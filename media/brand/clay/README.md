## The Happy Farmer (Jared, Nov 1 2026)
The clay farmer in scene-farmer-shelf.png, made from the Happy Seeds mascot logo (cream cowboy hat, red bandana, mustache, white shirt, blue overalls), is the heart of the brand and is called **the Happy Farmer**. He lives inside the world of the plants: in creatives he is small, 10 to 20 percent of the plants' height, so the plants show at full scale and tower over him, and he is always admiring or tending what is growing, never selling. Lead with him in future creatives and marketing assets. First approved examples: Canva media MAHZvL8CIN4 (tomato plant), MAHZvPsBV9s (windowsill kale and spinach).

## Happy Farmer World (Jared, Nov 2 2026): the brand look
Every creative, site image and email header is a stop-motion claymation scene with the Happy Farmer inside the world of the plants: small (10-20% of plant height), always tending or admiring (watering, sowing, patting, pulling, harvesting, cooking, eating), and ALWAYS with the small green red-throated hummingbird from the logo perched on his hat brim. Matte plasticine with thumb marks, never photoreal food. The box appears only when the idea is about the product. Site set lives in media/site/farmer/ (hero, herbs, sowing, gift, sprout-wide, email-banner), social statics in media/2026-11/statics/farmer-*.jpg, Canva designs DAHY1SD7IiY (statics) and DAHZvdUR5_Y (site/email). Every image is also archived in the hello@ Drive folder "Happy Seeds Farmer World Claymation Images" (17gHsmP3cSui1OFsnOXUOv-13Zl12jJmZ), titled by its description.

## The box (Jared, Nov 2 2026): always portrait
When the box appears it is ALWAYS portrait oriented (standing tall, never landscape). The drawer slides out of a SHORT end, the black pull tab is centered on that short end (never on a long side), and the ten packets and the grow guide are displayed in portrait inside it. Packets are multi-colored like the real product, no readable words. Current open-box image: media/site/farmer/farmer-inside.jpg (Canva MAHZxhqXxE8, DAHZvdUR5_Y page 11).
