Common questions

Is this good for total beginners?

It was built for them. Every variety comes with step-by-step instructions in the grow guide, and the ten vegetables were chosen for reliability, not difficulty.

Are the seeds non-GMO?

Yes. Every seed is non-GMO and open-pollinated, and every lot is lab tested, with the germination rate printed right on the packet.

Can I grow indoors or on a balcony?

Yes. The kit works in containers, balcony planters, raised beds, and backyard plots. The grow guide covers spacing and light for each variety.

When should I plant?

Spring for the full kit, and late summer is prime time to sow the cool-season five (kale, spinach, carrot, beet, turnip) for a fall harvest.
