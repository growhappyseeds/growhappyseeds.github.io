# City Apartment Growing series (daily)

Jared, Nov 3 2026: "start writing an extra blog daily about how to grow seeds in a city apartment with or without a balcony."

The SEO Writer writes ONE article from this list every day, on top of its regular Mon/Wed/Fri article from `topic-queue.md`. Same rules, front matter, review, `_pending/` parking and email approval as every other article (see `tools/seo/README.md`); nothing goes live until Jared replies APPROVED and the Executor publishes it.

How to use this file:
- Take the first unchecked `[ ]` topic. Alternate where you can between a "no balcony" topic and a "balcony" topic so the series serves both readers.
- When you park the draft, change `[ ]` to `[~]` and add "(drafted YYYY-MM-DD, pending)". Each Writer run changes `[~]` to `[x] live /blog/<slug>/` once that post is in sitemap.xml, and to `[!] held: <Jared's note>` once the file is in `content/blog/_rejected/`. The Content Run reads this file to know which live posts belong to the series.
- When fewer than 7 unchecked topics remain, add 10 new ones at the end (researched, real questions apartment growers ask, none overlapping a live post or a topic below).
- Every article in the series: opens with who it is for (no balcony, balcony, or both); names the apartment constraint (light, space, weight, wind, landlord, neighbors, heat from radiators) and gives a workable answer; links to at least one other series article once two are live, and to `/blog/best-vegetables-to-grow-on-a-balcony/` or `/blog/when-to-start-seeds-indoors/` where relevant. It never repeats what those two hand-built posts already cover (balcony sun hours, wind, pot sizes by crop, watering pots, north-facing balconies) beyond a one-line link.
- Product facts only from `content/brand.json`. What the kit honestly offers here: the five cool-season packets (kale, spinach, carrot, beet, turnip) grow in a sunny window or a balcony planter; the warm-season five (tomato, cucumber, pepper, eggplant, melon) want a big container and real sun, so a no-balcony reader starts them indoors and needs a balcony, a roof the building allows, or a community plot later. Say so plainly; never imply the whole kit grows on a windowsill. No grow lights, soil, pots or trays are in the box.
- Safety and rules: never recommend growing on a fire escape (it is an emergency exit and blocking it breaks fire code in most cities); tell balcony readers to check their lease or building rules and the balcony's weight limit for large wet containers.
