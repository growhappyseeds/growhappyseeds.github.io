    Common questions

    Is the Happy Grow Helper free?

    Yes. It is free for anyone growing vegetables from seed, whether or not you bought a Happy Seeds kit. You sign up with an email address so we can send stage reminders.

    Where are my plant photos stored?

    On your own device, in your browser's storage. Photos are never uploaded. Only the stage name and date are sent to Happy Seeds so your reminders arrive at the right time. Clearing your browser data removes them, so use the Share card to save a copy of your favorites.

    What milestones can I track?

    Seed planted, germination, sprout, first leaves, true leaves, flowering, fruit set, growing, ripening, harvest, cooking and eating, plus any custom milestone you add.

    Can I track more than one plant?

    Yes, as many as you like. Each plant has its own timeline and readiness meter.
